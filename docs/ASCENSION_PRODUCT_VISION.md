# ASCENSION product vision — owner confirmed November 2, 2026

This owner-confirmed vision supersedes older conflicting presentation notes. Preserve existing ASCENSION architecture and the approved Tree-and-Book experience; extend the app rather than replacing it.

The Tree is the star of the interface: prominent, alive and interactive. She is a warm, inviting woman-voiced collaborator who listens, remembers the objective and conversation, answers follow-ups, explains designs and makes relevant suggestions. She is not a passive narrator or a screen reader. A voice start callback alone is not proof of audible, natural playback.

Each pathway should embody a genuinely distinct possible creation. For a worker-bot objective, the pathways visually communicate distinct worker-bot approaches with shapes and tools reflecting their actual generated answers. BLEND communicates the useful combination. Keep four selectable pathways including BLEND; no count-heavy headings or internal engineering brief in the visible flow.

Selecting a pathway brings that SAME design into an interactive inspection space. No disconnected INVENTION PICTURE mode. No people, bystanders or decorative environments unless explicitly requested. The approved image, geometry and engineering description must remain coherent; a newly generated GLB is not automatically evidence of correspondence.

Component labels must locate the actual component: select the robotic arm, highlight that arm, focus the view and explain what it does, where it belongs and its connections beside the design. Do not invent component-to-geometry mappings or measurements. Unknown mappings remain unresolved.

Assembled, Blueprint, Dissect, Exploded/Imploded, zoom, rotation and reset examine one design rather than generate unrelated replacements. Blueprint must use established dimensions and connections where known and explicitly unresolved dimensions otherwise. A fused mesh is not a segmented mechanical assembly.

BUILD ON RESULT retains and extends the chosen design. Preview before DIVE INTO REALITY. Preserve Guardian, AutoSync, Knowledgeverse, Meaning Elements, identity and verification boundaries. Independent model contributions do not impersonate ASCENSION governed execution. No unwanted Lovable/Z.AI dependencies or replacement architecture.

Build toward this vision with separate implementation, test, live-runtime and owner-acceptance evidence. Do not call a deployed interface a finished invention engine. Do not mark older approval checklist boxes approved merely because this vision is confirmed.

## Crystal-orb pathways — owner correction, November 2

The pathways are the crystal orbs on the Tree, not rectangular cards placed beside it. Each orb is a window onto its own design, like peering through a crystal ball at a possible destiny. Move between the orbs, reveal the focused design's title/description without covering the Tree, and choose an orb to open the existing pathway inspection page.
